# Trademarks

The Pernce code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
The license covers the code, not the **Pernce name** or the **Pernce logo
and app icon** (the "P" with a coin, in `docs/design/icons/` and the
app's icon images). Section 6 of the license says so too: it grants no
rights to use the licensor's trademarks.

## What's fine

- Using, studying, modifying and running Pernce, including hosting your
  own sync server for yourself, your family or your organisation.
- Saying truthfully that your project is based on, compatible with, or a
  fork of Pernce ("Foo, a fork of Pernce").
- Using the name to talk about Pernce, in articles, reviews, tutorials or
  talks.
- Contributing to this repository, and keeping the name and icon in your
  fork while you develop changes to send back.

## What needs permission

- Publishing an app, build or hosted service under the Pernce name or with
  its logo or icon, for example on the App Store, Google Play or the web.
  If you distribute your own build, give it **a different name and icon**,
  and its own app identifiers (bundle ID, Android package, EAS project; see
  `mobile/app.json` and `mobile/app.config.ts`).
- Using the name or icon in a way that suggests your product is the
  official Pernce, or is endorsed by its maintainers.
- Using "Pernce" in the name of another product, company or domain.

To ask, open an issue or start a discussion on the repository.
