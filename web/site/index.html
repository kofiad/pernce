Import & export

A full JSON backup you can restore, and every transaction as CSV.

English & French

The whole app in either language.

Light and dark

Follows your system, or pick one in Settings.

Your money stays on your device.

Pernce stores everything locally, in this browser or on your phone. Nobody else holds a copy, including us.

Works offline

No connection needed. Install it from your browser and it opens like an app.

No account

Open it and start. There's nothing to sign up for and no server holding your money.

App lock

Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode before Pernce opens on your phone.

Encrypted sync, later

Syncing between devices is coming as an option, end-to-end encrypted: the server only sees ciphertext.

Open source, top to bottom.

The apps, the server and the money logic are all on GitHub under Apache 2.0. Read how your numbers are worked out, run it yourself, or open an issue.
